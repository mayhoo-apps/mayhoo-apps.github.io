Mayhoo privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy covers the Mayhoo apps: Mayhoo Hub, Mayhoo Cards, Mayhoo Rummy, Mayhoo Solitaire, Mayhoo Puzzles and Mayhoo Casino, whether you get them from Google Play or from our GitHub downloads.

Short version: we don't collect any data about you. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The Mayhoo apps don't ask you to sign in, don't show ads, and don't include analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. The versions on Google Play don't connect to the internet at all.

What stays on your phone

Your saved games, settings, scores, win and loss records, daily puzzle streaks and Casino play-money balance are kept in the app's own storage on your device. We can't see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you turn on Android backup, Android may copy them to your own Google account. That is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us.

Between Mayhoo apps

Mayhoo Hub can share the table look you choose (background and card back) with other Mayhoo apps on the same phone. This stays on your device.

Links to other sites

Mayhoo Hub can open Google Play pages for other Mayhoo apps. Google Play's own privacy policy applies there.

GitHub downloads

The versions downloaded from our GitHub releases also connect to GitHub to check for and download updates. GitHub receives the request like any website visit (for example your IP address); we don't send anything about you or your games. These versions also have a Sponsor button that opens Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors in your browser if you tap it. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The Mayhoo apps aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email support@mayhoo.com, or open an issue on GitHub.
